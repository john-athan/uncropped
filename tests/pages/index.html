Uncropped: test pages

Three page shapes. Capture each one and check the stated result.

01   PLAIN ARTICLE The document scrolls, the way a blog does. Sticky header, lazy images below the fold.
Expect: tall image, header once at the top, no blank image boxes.
 02   APP SHELL html and body are one viewport tall. A div does the scrolling. This is the case DevTools gets wrong.
Expect: tall image of the panel only, no sidebar repeated down the side.
 03   WIDE TABLE Wider than the window and taller than it, so the capture has to tile on both axes.
Expect: one wide, tall image, no seam between columns.
